Why Dependency-Aware Kanban?

Standard boards assume tasks are independent. Real engineering projects are interconnected.

Traditional Kanban

  • ❌ Blocked tasks can be moved into In Progress prematurely.
  • ❌ Dependencies stay hidden inside descriptions and comments.
  • ❌ Circular dependencies can break workflow execution.
  • ❌ Teams must manually check whether blockers have cleared.

FlowLock Graph-Kanban

  • ✔ Smart Task Locking: blocked work cannot move forward.
  • ✔ Dependency Graph: see relationships between tasks.
  • ✔ Cycle Detection: DFS prevents circular dependencies.
  • ✔ Auto Unlock: completing blockers unlocks dependents.
